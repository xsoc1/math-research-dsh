## Reproduced packaging defect

STORE fixed-source policy Commit
`eab44d15c250644a6db2e1ae8b4fa43e972097a2` treats negative `files` selectors as
unsupported. Version 2.0.2 declared `!skills/**/__pycache__/**`, `.pyc` and `.pyo`
exclusions. Its scanner therefore fell back to the entire checkout: 240 files,
109 scannable sources, including development tests that introduced credential
and dynamic-module signals.

Version 2.0.3 generates an exact positive list from the frozen resource lock.
Synchronization refreshes it; Bundle and archive gates reject drift. All 140
research resources remain byte-equivalent after LF normalization. The distributable
surface is 147 files, with 59 scannable sources. Checkout-only source, fixture and
development files are no longer accidentally classified as installed runtime.
The archive gate also checks exact membership and each locked resource hash.

Tests cover the old ambiguous manifest, resource-list refresh in an isolated
preview, paths escaping the resource root, glob injection and generated bytecode.
They also require executable helpers and Lean templates to remain visible.
